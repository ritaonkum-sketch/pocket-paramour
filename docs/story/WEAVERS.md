# POCKET PARAMOUR — WEAVERS

*Quick-reference for what Soul Weavers are, what they do, and how the
magic works on the page.*

For the full lineage and timeline, see `docs/story/TIMELINE.md`.
For the full canon, see `LORE.md` and `docs/story/STORY-BIBLE.md`.

---

## 🧵 What a Weaver is

A **Soul Weaver** (or just **Weaver**) is a rare individual who can do
*one specific thing no one else in this world can do:*

**They read THE DEEP and weave it into bonds between people.**

- **The Deep** = the raw underground magic beneath Aethermoor. Older
  than the kingdom built on top of it. Pure, wild, unusable directly by
  ordinary people. It is the magical *bones* of the world.
- A Weaver can *touch* The Deep and *translate* it into **bond-magic**
  — the surface form. Bonds between people, places, songs, kingdoms.
- Without Weavers, The Deep remains untranslated. It leaks up unwoven
  as **wounds** — dark magic, wound-creatures, the rot.

**A Weaver is not the strength. A Weaver is the *translation layer.***

---

## 🪡 What a Weaver does (the work, mechanically)

The bonds a Weaver weaves become **the bone-structure of the kingdom.**
Specifically:

- Streets exist because someone *trusted* a builder.
- Walls stand because someone *protected* a city.
- Magic flows because someone *loved* someone.
- Sirens sing the caves intact because their song *binds* the stone.
- The wards on Lucien's tower hold because his discipline *bound* them
  for forty years.

Every bond a Weaver weaves is a thread in the bond-fabric. The thicker
the fabric, the more *whole* the world is. The thinner it becomes, the
more the world *thins* — buildings go unsound, people distrust each
other, magic dies in the wards.

**A Weaver's tools are their hands, their voice, their presence, and
other people's love.** Not spells. Not incantations. Bonds.

---

## 🌍 Where Weavers come from

**Weavers are not born in Aethermoor.** They are only ever found in
*other kingdoms.* This is the rule, locked.

For centuries, Aethermoor has *imported* Weavers via political
marriage. By Aethermoor law, **marrying a Weaver gives the spouse a
legitimate claim to the throne** (the *Weaver-Marriage Law*). It is the
kingdom's deepest structural mechanism — and its deepest vulnerability.

**Exception:** Proto, born in Aethermoor against the rule (~150 yrs
ago). Why is an open canon mystery.

---

## 🌟 How rare

**Seven Weavers have ever existed in the kingdom's known history.**
Across roughly 650 years.

> *"A Weaver is not made. A Weaver is found. The world is not generous
> with them."*

For the seven by name and timeline, see `TIMELINE.md`.

## 👤 What the heroine is (specifically)

She is the **Seventh Weaver.** Born daughter of a Duke of **Caerwyn**
(destroyed kingdom — see `LORE.md §1.1`).

Her specific gift, distinct from the others: **she bonds easily,
widely, across many people.** Range, not depth. Where Wren (the Fourth)
tried to save everyone and burned out, the heroine can hold many bonds
without dying *if she paces herself.*

**Each affection tier-up the heroine reaches with a love interest =
healing one wound in the bond-fabric of the world.**

That's why the game IS the plot. The romance isn't parallel to the
saving-the-world arc; **it IS the saving-the-world arc.** Bonding with
the love interests densely enough to outpace Aenor's consumption is
*how she saves the kingdom.*

**No combat. No spells. Loving people densely is the work.**

---

## ⚖️ What Aenor is, by contrast

**Aenor is not a Weaver.** She has only the diluted royal bond-magic.
Everything she has, she *stole* — by consuming Weavers.

Each Weaver Aenor consumed left a wound where they used to be weaving
(a part of the bond-fabric they were maintaining went unwoven). Six
wounds for six centuries. The wounds spread.

> **Weavers bond. Aenor consumes.**

That's the structural opposition the whole story is built on. They are
the same magic running in opposite directions.

## 📖 What we've seen on the page (actual Weaver magic in scenes)

### Ch10 — Lyra's loom (the first lesson)

Lyra showed the heroine *"the smallest thing a Weaver does."* She put
the heroine's hand on a small bone-and-thread loom, and the heroine
felt **a triangle of warmth** — *Lyra's hand → her hand → the cloth →
back into Lyra.*

Lyra's words:
> *"A loom is a thing made of thread. A person is a thing made of
> bonds. The kingdom is a thing made of more bonds than you can count.
> You will, eventually, feel a kingdom the way you just felt the loom."*

### Ch32 — the first conscious Weaver act on a person

The heroine's hand on Elian's shoulder at the two graves. **The
seventh-light came up under her palm without her asking** — the same
colour the loom had been in the cave. The light *"went into him. Found
the place his grief lives. Began to do something there."*

She wove on his grief. **This is the first time she has used the gift
on a person consciously.** Elian's response: he placed his hand over
hers and let her.

### The pattern this establishes

- The light is warm, gentle, with the colour of the loom-light in the
  cave (cyan-toward-lavender — the colour of a Weaver's translation).
- It *finds* the wound or the grief on its own — the heroine does not
  aim it. The body knows.
- It does not consume. It *weaves on.* It mends or holds or settles.
- It is small at first. The bigger workings (kingdom-scale weaving)
  come later, with practice and care-arc depth.

## 🛤 The two routes her power can take

| Route | What she does | What it costs |
|---|---|---|
| **LIGHT** | Bonds densely with the men she loves. The light from her hands heals wounds. Each bond restitches the fabric. | The world is saved; her own kingdom (Caerwyn) stays gone. The sacrifice of home for the world. |
| **DARK-LEANING** *("The Weaver-Queen Returns")* | She consumes selectively. Adopts some of Aenor's methods. Returns to Caerwyn and rebuilds it from ashes. | She becomes a corrupted Weaver. Her soul has a shadow on it. She walks closer to Aenor than the light route allows. |

The player's care-route over the spine decides which.
