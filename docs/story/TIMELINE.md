## 🔗 The major events, in order

### ~650 years ago — THE SELENE TRAGEDY
- Selene brought from another kingdom to marry **Aelric** (Aenor's older
  brother, the original heir of Aethermoor)
- Selene and Aenor fell in love (queer, unspoken — the Aethermoor court
  had no language for it)
- **Aelric attempted fratricide** out of jealousy
- Selene died saving Aenor → Aenor accidentally consumed her bond-light
- **Aelric stepped aside in shame → AENOR BECAME QUEEN**
- Aenor vowed *"never again"* — burned every record of Selene's name,
  kept Selene's ring

### ~600 years ago — THE VEYRA CATASTROPHE
*(50 years after Selene)*
- Veyra was a noble of Nocthera. As a teen she had been **Elian's first
  love** — secret, forbidden, in the Thornwood
- Years later, betrothed to Prince Corvin Noctalis. Their political
  engagement became real love.
- **Cassien** (a Duke of Aethermoor from a cadet branch of the royal
  house) brought Veyra to Aethermoor to marry her under the
  Weaver-Marriage Law and **overthrow Aenor**
- Aenor met Veyra and tried to convince her to *"join hands"* — Aenor
  wanted Veyra as her Weaver-partner (queer-coded offer)
- Veyra refused both. She wanted Corvin.
- Corvin came to Aethermoor → **magical duel with Aenor**
- Aenor **consumed Veyra on the spot**, gained her power, defeated
  Corvin, **sealed him**
- Elian felt Veyra's consumption via the forest-whisper, found her
  body, **buried her at the rowan** in the Thornwood
- **Cassien — calculating — proposed an alliance with Aenor**; she
  accepted. They shared a bed politically; no natural child.

### ~599 years ago
- **Nocthera collapsed** — one generation after Corvin was sealed. The
  bond-fabric of the kingdom died. The land became bone.

### ~550 years ago
- **Cassien died.** Officially *"a long illness."* Buried in the royal
  vault beneath the cathedral.

### ~400-500 years ago — the Third Weaver
- Sealed her own daughter to hide her from Aenor; the act broke her
  mind. *"Walked the inland road and was lost to the wood."*

### ~250 years ago — WREN, the Fourth Weaver
- **Aenor burned Wren's village and her kingdom** in her hunt for the
  hidden Weaver. **Wren was the only survivor.** A child or young teen
  when her home went under, she wandered alone for years.
- Eventually walked into the **Thornwood** uninvited. She had nothing
  left to lose; she had no one to belong to. She did not ask Elian's
  permission.
- **Elian's second buried love.** He told her on the first night what
  she was — believing the warning would let him be quick enough this
  time.
- The warning made her flee. Fleeing made her catchable. *(Her body
  already knew how to flee — she had fled once before, from her
  village.)*
- The kingdom took her at 30, in 5 years instead of 10.
- **Knowing made it worse.** This is the central wound-shape of Elian's
  arc.
- *(Wren is the heroine's mirror — same kingdom-destruction by Aenor,
  same sole-survivor fate, but Wren had no parents and no rift. The
  parallel is a future-arc gut-punch when the heroine learns of her.)*

### ~200 years ago — the Fifth Weaver
- Fled the centring. Fate unknown.

### ~150 years ago — THE NIGHT
*The single most important night in the spine. Everything traces here.*

1. **Aenor's forces close on Caerwyn** hunting the hidden Weaver child
   (the heroine)
2. The heroine's parents + **the Siren (Lyra's mother)** + **the High
   Mage (Lucien's father)** cast forbidden magic → **tear the rift**
3. The heroine (age 14) is sent through with **The First Weave** →
   lands in the modern world → loses memory
4. **Proto** flees into the rift → Aenor catches him sideways →
   **mis-consumes him**
5. **Aenor's decay begins** (from the incomplete consumption)
6. The Siren and the High Mage **met that night** → later, **Lyra and
   Lucien are born**
7. **Caerwyn is destroyed** by Aenor anyway → the heroine's parents
   die

### ~150 yrs ago → ~2 yrs ago — the long decay
- Aenor's decay slowly corrodes her oldest magic
- **Noir's 600-year seal rots** as the decay reaches it
- **Noir wakes inside the seal ~150 yrs ago** (when the decay began) —
  he is conscious in a corroding cage for 150 years

### ~80 years ago — THE CONSTRUCTED HEIR
- Aenor's decay accelerates. She panics about her own aging.
- She **digs up Cassien's grave** with her own hands
- Weaves **Aurelius** (Caspian's father) from his bones, her own
  essence, and undigested bond-fragments
- Aurelius is born; fully mortal but necromantic in origin

### ~25 years ago
- **Caspian is born** to Aurelius and Lirien

### ~15 years ago — THE BRIDGE
- Aurelius and Lirien try to flee Aenor with Caspian (10) and Mira (4)
- Aenor's riders catch them
- **Aurelius, Lirien, and Mira die.** Caspian survives.
- *(The hidden brother — a secret third child — is born around this
  time and hidden far away by Lirien's old nurse)*

### ~2 years before Ch1
- The heroine, in the modern world, **opens The First Weave** → the
  silver thread appears → she is pulled back through the rift
- Her arrival as a living Weaver (the first in 150 years) resonates
  Noir's rotted seal apart → **NOIR WALKS FREE**
- The heroine hides 2 years; The First Weave is **taken** from her and
  confiscated to Lucien's tower

### Recent — pre-Ch1
- The heroine finds the Book in Lucien's tower
- **Rips out the seal-page** → 600 years of bound magic detonates →
  flung across the kingdom to the south Thornwood → **amnesia #2**

### Ch1 — present day
- The heroine wakes in the moss with the burnt seal-page in her hand
