# PART I · THE CANON

## §1 · The World & The Magic

### The Deep

Beneath Aethermoor lies **THE DEEP** — a vein of raw, pure magic that
runs through the underground stone. The Deep is older than the kingdom
built on top of it. It cannot be used directly by ordinary people; it is
too wild, too pure. **It is the magical bones of the world.**

### Bond-magic & the Weavers

The Deep can only be *read and woven* by **Soul Weavers** — rare
individuals who can take the raw underground magic and *weave it into
bonds between people.* The bonds are the bond-magic; the bond-magic is
the surface-usable form. Together they form what holds the kingdom
together: streets exist because someone trusted a builder; walls stand
because someone protected a city; magic flows because someone loved
someone. **Weaving is the work that translates The Deep into a livable
world.**

- When bonds are strong, the kingdom is whole.
- When bonds thin, the world thins.
- When The Deep is unwoven, it leaks up — **as dark magic, as wounds.**

### Weavers are not born in Aethermoor

**Weavers can only be found outside Aethermoor.** They are never born
within its borders. The Aethermoor lineage carries the diluted royal
bond-magic — not the Weaver gift. For centuries, Aethermoor has
*imported* Weavers from other kingdoms via political marriage. This is
the kingdom's deepest structural mechanism, and its deepest
vulnerability.

*(Exception: see §9 — Proto, born in Aethermoor, the rare exception
that proves the rule. The mystery of how is an open canon question.)*

### The Weaver-Marriage Law

By the law of Aethermoor, **marrying a Weaver gives the spouse a
legitimate claim to the throne.** This single statute is the engine of
much of the kingdom's history.

- Marrying a Weaver = legitimacy.
- Marrying a Weaver = threat to whoever currently holds the throne.
- Marrying a Weaver = political power.

This is why Aethermoor's nobility compete fiercely for Weaver-marriages;
this is why imported Weavers are the kingdom's most contested figures;
this is why **Aenor must control, marry, or consume any Weaver who
enters her kingdom.**

### Why the kingdom is collapsing now

For 150 years, Aenor has consumed every Weaver who entered Aethermoor.
The import pipeline has dried up — the other kingdoms have run out of
Weavers willing to be brought to Aethermoor, and Aenor's hunger has
continued anyway. **The Deep is no longer being woven.** It leaks up as
wounds. The eastern wood is sick. Towns past the headland are bones.
The wards are thinning. The kingdom is becoming what was beneath it:
raw, unwoven, ruined.

### The Kingdoms

- **Aethermoor** — the surviving kingdom. Ruled by Queen Aenor. Built
  above The Deep. Importer of Weavers.
- **Nocthera** — Corvin's kingdom. Fell 600 years ago when its heir was
  sealed. Now bone.
- **Caerwyn** — the heroine's birth kingdom. Far from Aethermoor.
  **Destroyed by Aenor ~150 years ago in her hunt for the hidden Weaver
  child.** Now ruins. *(The kingdom the heroine may, in one route,
  return to and rebuild — see §10.)*
- **The modern world** — where the heroine grew up. Time runs thin
  there: **~7 modern years ≈ 150 fantasy years.**
- **The rift** — a tear between worlds, opened on THE NIGHT.
- **Dark magic** — The Deep, unwoven. The wounds left where Weavers were
  consumed. Absence-black smoke. Wound-creatures.

## §2 · THE NIGHT — the engine of the whole game

One night, ~150 years ago, in the doomed kingdom of **Caerwyn**:

1. Aenor's forces close in. Caerwyn is burning. They are hunting the
   hidden Weaver child.
2. The heroine's parents bring together **the Siren (Lyra's mother)**
   and **the High Mage (Lucien's father)** — strangers before that
   night — to cast ancient, forbidden magic. It **tears the rift.**
3. They send the heroine (age 14) through with **The First Weave.** Meant
   for a safe world — she lands in **ours.** Her memory is lost.
4. The raw rift becomes a hiding place → **Proto**, the hunted 6th Weaver,
   flees into it.
5. Aenor, snatching at Proto as he escapes, catches him wrong →
   **mis-consumes him** → her **decay begins.**
6. 150 years of decay rot her oldest magic → **Noir's 600-year seal
   corrodes → Noir wakes** inside his cage.
7. The Siren and the High Mage met that night → later, **Lyra and Lucien
   are born.**

**One act of parental love set all of it in motion** — the heroine's
exile, Proto's survival, Aenor's decay, Noir's eventual freedom, and the
very existence of two love interests. Every Act One mystery traces here.

## §4 · The Heroine — locked origin

A true **7th Weaver**, born daughter of a **high Duke** of **Caerwyn**.
Court-raised to age 14 — **this is why her body knows royal dance and
etiquette: it is her real childhood, surviving the amnesia.**

- Her parents hid her Weaver nature from birth. They knew Aenor hunts
  Weavers kingdom to kingdom — coercing rulers to surrender them,
  destroying the ones who refuse.
- Aenor learned a Weaver was hidden in Caerwyn. She came. **She broke
  the kingdom hunting the heroine.**
- **THE NIGHT (~150 yrs ago):** her parents + the Siren + the High Mage
  tear the rift and send her through with **The First Weave.** Meant
  for a safe world — she lands in **ours.** The crossing takes her
  memory (**amnesia #1** — her fantasy childhood).
- She grows up a modern orphan girl. No memory of Weaver, kingdom,
  parents. She finds love there. The Book stays with her, unreadable.
- **~2 yrs before Ch1:** she opens The First Weave → the **silver thread**
  appears → she touches it → pulled home. Her arrival, a living Weaver
  after 150 years of none, resonates Noir's rotted seal apart. **Noir
  walks free.**
- She hides 2 years (modern memories intact; Weaver-origin still gone).
  The Book is **taken** from her — confiscated to the mage tower.
- She tracks it down. Recently, in Lucien's tower, she **rips out the
  seal-page** → 600 yrs of bound Weaver-magic detonates → she is flung
  across the kingdom to the south Thornwood (**amnesia #2** — even her
  modern life, gone).
- **Ch1:** she wakes in the moss, a total blank, the burnt seal-page in
  her hand.

**Double amnesia:** #2 (modern life) is the shallower layer, peels first;
#1 (fantasy origin) is the deeper layer beneath.

## §5 · The First Weave — the Book

The one master grimoire of Weaver magic. It holds:

- the **discovery of the first Weaver power** — the origin of bond-magic;
- every **secret** of Weaving — how Aenor consumes, how the rift works,
  how the world can be re-woven;
- recorded binding-spells — **including part of Noir's seal.**

Dense with live magic; ripping a page is violent. **It cannot simply be
read — it reveals itself only as the reader's own Weaver power grows.**
Sigil: two crossed branches and a moon (the old Weaver order's mark).
Originated in the old mage tower; stolen by the heroine's parents on THE
NIGHT. Aenor has hunted it for 150 years. **Currently in Lucien's
tower** — confiscated as a dangerous artifact; Lucien has been studying
it and cannot read it (it is Weaver-magic; he is a mage).

## §6 · Aenor — the Queen

- **Not a Weaver.** Everything she has, she stole.
- **Queer.** Her preference has always been for women. In a kingdom that
  required heirs and bound queens to political marriage, she could not
  love openly.
- **Younger sister of AELRIC**, the original heir of Aethermoor. She
  became queen only after Aelric stepped aside (see §7).

### ~650 yrs ago — THE SELENE TRAGEDY

- **Selene** was brought from another kingdom to marry Aenor's brother
  **Aelric** (a political Weaver-marriage, the standard Aethermoor
  arrangement under the Weaver-marriage law).
- During the courtship, **Selene and Aenor fell in love.** Aenor was
  young, untrained, and queer; it was the first deep love either of
  them had had.
- **Aelric grew jealous** — frightened of losing his bride to his
  sister. He could not openly accuse them; the love between the two
  women was the kind of love that had no court-language.
- **Aelric attempted an "accident"** to kill Aenor.
- Selene saw the danger and **threw herself between Aenor and death.**
- Aenor — young, untrained, in lethal panic — her wild power lashed out
  and **accidentally consumed Selene's bond-light to save herself.**
- Selene died in Aenor's arms. Aenor wept for days. **Burned every
  record of Selene's name.** Kept the ring Selene gave her. **Vowed
  never again.**
- Aelric — knowing what he had caused, terrified of his sister's new
  power — **stepped aside in shame.** Aenor became Queen.
- *(Aelric's fate post-abdication is an open canon slot — see §11.)*

### ~600 yrs ago — THE VEYRA CATASTROPHE

- Veyra was a noble of Nocthera. As a young teen she had been **Elian's
  first love** in the Thornwood (forbidden, secret, because she was
  Nocthera royalty and he was a wood-warden).
- Years later she was politically betrothed to Prince Corvin Noctalis.
  Their political engagement became real love.
- **Cassien**, a Duke of Aethermoor from a cadet branch of the royal
  house, sought to marry Veyra under the Weaver-marriage law — *if he
  succeeded, he could legally overthrow Aenor.*
- Cassien brought Veyra to Aethermoor. **Aenor met her too and tried to
  convince Veyra to "join hands" with her** — Aenor wanted Veyra as her
  Weaver-partner. *The offer was queer-coded, the way her love for
  Selene had been.*
- Veyra refused both. She wanted Corvin.
- Corvin came to Aethermoor to protect her. He and Aenor **fought with
  magic.** Catastrophic.
- To win, Aenor **consumed Veyra's bond-light on the spot** in the
  duel. She gained Veyra's power. She defeated Corvin and **sealed
  him** (see §8).
- Veyra's body fell — drained but intact.
- **Cassien** — witnessing all of it, *calculating* — saw that Aenor
  was now the strongest piece on the board. He proposed an **alliance:**
  he would marry her, bring his cadet-branch royal legitimacy and
  military strength, and reign at her side. **Aenor accepted.** They
  shared a bed because the alliance required it. They **never had a
  child naturally** (Aenor's queer; possibly her newly-stolen magic
  made her infertile; the political marriage was duty, not love).
- Across the kingdom, **Elian felt Veyra's consumption** via the
  forest-whisper, followed it, found her body where it had fallen.
  *No one else had come for her.* He carried her to the rowan in his
  wood and buried her — the first stone.

### The pattern continues

- **Has consumed every Weaver since.** Coerces kingdoms to surrender
  their Weavers; destroys those who refuse — rarely; coercion is her
  way.
- **~150 yrs ago:** mis-consumed Proto → her **decay** began. **~80 yrs
  ago**, panicking at her own aging, she **dug up Cassien's bones from
  the cathedral vault** and used them to weave herself an heir,
  **Aurelius** (see §7).
- **Now:** her decay accelerates. She *needs to consume the 7th Weaver*
  to survive.
- **The trap:** her hunger has perversely *contained* some wounds. Kill
  her carelessly and they all tear open at once. She must be **unwoven
  carefully** — only a Weaver can.
- **Ending: tragic fall only.** No redemption. Her single possible
  grace: saying *"Selene"* aloud once, at the very end.
- **She has spent six centuries destroying the women she could have
  loved.**

## §7 · The Aethermoor Royal Line

**The Old King** (Aenor and Aelric's father) → **AELRIC** (older
brother; original heir; **stepped aside ~650 yrs ago after the Selene
tragedy he caused**) → **AENOR** (became queen by ascension).

**CASSIEN** — from a cadet branch of the royal house (descended from
the younger brother of an earlier king). A Duke. Royal blood close
enough for the throne-claim mechanic, far enough that marriage to
Aenor was not incestuous. **Brought Veyra to overthrow Aenor; lost her
in the duel; allied with Aenor afterward.** Shared her bed politically;
no natural child. Died ~550 years ago. **His grave was robbed by
Aenor ~80 years ago** for his bones.

**AURELIUS** (Caspian's father) — **woven by Aenor from Cassien's bones**
~70 yrs ago. Fully mortal once born, but necromantic in origin. Turned
out kind, which disappointed her.

Married **LIRIEN** (Aethermoor minor nobility, a love match).

**CASPIAN** (~25, crown prince, named for Cassien — Aurelius insisted
on the name without knowing why).

- **MIRA** — Caspian's younger sister. Killed at the Bridge ~15 yrs ago
  when Aurelius and Lirien tried to flee Aenor with their children and
  were caught by her riders. Aurelius and Lirien died too. Caspian,
  age 10, the only survivor.
- **The hidden brother** — a secret third child, ~5 now, hidden from
  court as a stillbirth. Neither Caspian nor Aenor knows he lives.
  (Future-arc slot.)

## §8 · Noir — Prince Corvin Noctalis

Heir of fallen **Nocthera**. **Sealed 600 years ago by Aenor in the
magical duel** where she consumed Veyra. **Awake inside the seal since
~150 years ago** — when Aenor's decay began; 150 years conscious in a
corroding cage. **Walked free ~2 years before Ch1** — the heroine's
arrival as a living Weaver finished the rotted seal. He now hides in
fallen Nocthera and slips into Aethermoor to gather intel, locate
Aenor, and plan **revenge.** His arc fork: revenge, or a future.

---

## §9 · Proto — the Sixth Weaver (the Exception)

The rule: Weavers are not born in Aethermoor. **Proto is the
exception.** Born within Aethermoor's borders ~150 years ago — the rare
case the kingdom should not have produced. A **wardwright** by trade
(one who worked on the kingdom's wards). His existence was a quiet
mystery the mage tradition kept; Aenor came for him before his
home-grown Weaver-status could destabilize the import-marriage
politics.

Lives in **the rift.** Fought Aenor ~150 years ago, fled into the
rift on THE NIGHT, survives there — *"a wound she stopped checking
on."* Carries the echoes of the five Weavers before him. **Monitors the
heroine across the rift** — the **silver thread** is the rift's edge
bleeding through into Aethermoor wherever she is. Golden-retriever
warm. Reachable early as a seam-whisper; meets her in person around
Ch20.

*(The mystery of why Aethermoor produced him at all is open — see
§11.)*

## §10 · The other four love interests

- **ALISTAIR** — guard captain, common-born. Arc: duty → love.
- **ELIAN** — warden of the Thornwood, older than he looks. **Veyra was
  his first love** (secret, when they were both young, when she was
  Nocthera nobility slipping into his wood and he was a young
  wood-warden; their love could not be made public). After Veyra was
  consumed in Aenor's duel with Corvin, **he found her body where it
  fell in the wood and buried her at the rowan — because no one else
  had come for her.** Many years later he loved a second Weaver (the
  Fourth) and buried her too. Fear-driven now: afraid he will fail a
  third.
- **LYRA** — the last siren; her people were massacred by Aenor's
  forces. Her mother — **the Siren** — helped cast the rift on THE
  NIGHT and later died in the persecution. Lyra is Lucien's
  half-sister.
- **LUCIEN** — a hollow scholar-mage, no human emotion until the
  heroine. Lyra's half-brother. His father — **the High Mage** —
  helped cast the rift on THE NIGHT. He holds The First Weave in his
  tower.

# PART II · THE WORKING ARC

## Act One — "The Weaver Wakes" (Ch1-30, exists in script)

She wakes, meets all six love interests, learns fragments of what she
is. The Queen decides to move. The hunt is sprung. Marra dies. Caspian
breaks. Lucien commits. Alistair rides. The heroine grieves in Elian's
hut while Proto watches from the rift's edge.

## Act Two — "The Fracture"

Aenor hunts the heroine (she must consume the 7th Weaver to halt her
decay). The heroine learns to weave. Each love interest is forced to
choose **crown or Weaver.** Noir's revenge surfaces. Routes begin to
diverge by the player's care. **The crown fractures** — Caspian refuses
to surrender her, Alistair has broken his oath. **War begins as a
succession crisis.**

## Act Three — "The War & The Rift"

A **three-way war:** Queen's loyalists / the Weaver's alliance / the rot
itself. Nocthera's restoration begins. Noir's revenge reaches its fork.
The confrontation with Aenor — and **the Selene reveal.** Aenor falls.

## Act Four — Endings (kept open — the player's care-route decides)

**Two endgame routes for the heroine:**

### The Light Route
She saves Aethermoor and Nocthera. Anchors to her chosen LI(s). Her
own birth kingdom — **Caerwyn** — stays gone. The sacrifice of home
for the world. Pure Weaver path.

### The Dark-Leaning Route — *"The Weaver-Queen Returns"*
She consumes selectively, becoming a **corrupted Weaver.** Adopts some
of Aenor's methods to defeat her. **Returns to Caerwyn and rebuilds it
from ashes**, reigning there as Weaver-Queen. She becomes someone
harder, scarred, *not the same Weaver she could have been.* The line
between her and Aenor narrows. Caerwyn breathes again; her soul has a
shadow on it.

Plus the LI-specific dark routes already in the codebase (Noir's
Completion, Lucien's Cold System, Caspian's Throne, Alistair's Broken
Oath, etc.).

## How the War Begins

A succession crisis, not an invasion. Aenor moves to seize the heroine
→ Caspian refuses → Alistair breaks his oath → the court splits into
queen's faction and prince's faction → Noir's Nocthera-faction enters
from outside → the rot becomes a third side. **A war of loyalty before
it is a war of armies.**

## How Noir's Revenge Goes

He has 600 years of hate and 2 years of planning. But a careless kill
of Aenor releases every wound her hunger has been containing — it would
destroy the world Veyra died loving. Veyra's memory and the heroine's
bond pull him from *raze* toward *rebuild.* His route ending hinges on
which he chooses.

## How Weaver-Magic Saves The Three Kingdoms

- **Aethermoor:** the heroine weaves enough living bonds to outpace
  Aenor's hunger and the rot. The Deep is woven again. The kingdom
  steadies.
- **Nocthera:** she weaves bonds *into* the dead kingdom, with **Noir**
  as its living anchor — the bone-land breathes again.
- **Caerwyn:** her own home kingdom is harder. *Light route:* it stays
  in ruins. *Dark-leaning route:* she returns, becomes corrupted in
  the doing, rebuilds it, reigns there at moral cost.
- **The rift:** healing it is the keystone — and Proto, who lives
  inside it, is her hands on the far side.
